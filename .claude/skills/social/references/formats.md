# Formats and mechanics

## Why this account can work

Pet Instagram is saturated and cute is not a differentiator. Three things here
are, and every post should be doing one of them.

1. **The honest record.** Almost every puppy account posts the highlight reel.
   This journal has "a sofa-biting rethink" and "two minutes at a time" in it.
   Setbacks are what make someone in the same week save a post, and saves are
   the strongest ranking signal there is.
2. **A time series nobody can fake.** Dated entries from day one mean "week 1
   and week 6, same spot" is available forever and gets more valuable every
   week. An account starting today cannot produce it for six weeks. It is the
   only thing here that compounds.
3. **Lessons.** A "what I learned" note is the most saveable format on the
   platform, and it is the evidence base for ever selling anything. Photos
   build an audience. Lessons build the standing to offer that audience
   something.

## The five pillars

Keep roughly to these so the grid has a shape and the algorithm can tell what
the account is about.

| Pillar | Source | Format | Why it earns its place |
|---|---|---|---|
| The daily record | Any journal entry | Reel or single photo | The honest week-by-week, setbacks left in |
| Then vs now | Two entries, same subject | Comparison card or split reel | The compounding moat |
| Firsts | A "first" sentence in an entry | Reel | Emotional peak, highest share rate |
| What I learned | `src/lessons/*.md` | Carousel | Saves, and the route to a product |
| Just the dog | Best photo of the week | Single photo | Not everything has to be a lesson |

The last row matters. An account where every post teaches something is
exhausting. Roughly one in five should just be the dog.

## The series this account already has

The pillars above are the shape. These are the actual recurring formats, and
every one of them came out of the journal rather than off a list of ideas.
Reach for these before inventing a new one.

### Chill Annie / Baby Shark Annie

The single best asset here, and the owner already named it (29 Aug). A puppy
with two named modes and a visible tip-over point between them is a
*character*, and people follow characters, not dogs. Every other account has a
cute puppy; almost none has one the audience can predict and root for.

Format: the hard cut. Calm, then the exact frame where she goes. Split screen
also works. It is funny, it is universally recognised by anyone with a puppy,
and it is infinitely repeatable without ever repeating itself, because the
tip-over looks different every week.

Runs forever. Use it when there is nothing else.

### The tip-over

The serious half of the same observation: spotting the moment she goes from
settled to overtired-and-biting, and what to do about it. The journal keeps
circling this (24, 25, 29, 30 Aug) because it is the real daily problem.

Almost nobody makes good content about this and every new owner is living it.
That is a saved post, which is the strongest signal there is.

### Controlling the environment

The owner's own recurring phrase, across at least three entries. It is a
thesis, not a tip: the playpen, the mats, the crate door reopening, standing up
and turning away instead of telling her off, taking her upstairs when guests
made the room uncontrollable.

This is the spine of whatever the owner eventually teaches other people, so it
is the series that matters most in the long run even though it will not be the
one that travels furthest this week.

### The honest failure

The counterweight, and the reason to trust the account. First poo indoors after
a good run. Weeing on the mats that were put down to stop mud. "You get lulled
into a false sense of security." Missing most of an evening with friends
because someone had to manage the dog, and saying plainly that this is the
trade.

Post these. They cost nothing and they are the difference between an account
people like and an account people believe.

### Small numbers getting bigger

The arc only someone recording from day one can show: 30 seconds alone, then
two minutes, then out of the house and down the driveway. Nothing dramatic in
any single step, which is exactly the point, and it is proof the method works.

Pairs with the then-vs-now card in `card.js`.

### Nerding out

The owner built a skill matrix for grading her progress and is reading his way
through the books. A systems-minded person raising a dog properly is a specific
identity and a rare one in this niche.

Note carefully: this is the series that sits nearest the AI story, and it still
does not mention it. It is about the person's way of thinking, not about the
tools. See `voice.md`.

## Platform mechanics

Verified August 2026. These are the reasons the package has the fields it has,
so re-check them if something stops working.

- **Reels are the reach format.** 7 to 30 seconds. Anything over 3 minutes
  stops being recommended to non-followers.
- **Ranking signals, strongest first: saves, shares, DMs, comments, likes.**
  Write for the save. "I needed this" beats "aww" every time.
- **Engagement velocity in the first ~30 minutes** counts for more than total
  engagement. Fifty saves in half an hour outranks five hundred over three
  days. This is why the daily routine is 1 minute posting and 9 minutes
  replying, and why posting and then walking away wastes the post.
- **Five hashtags maximum**, platform-enforced since Dec 2025. They are
  metadata now, not a reach multiplier. Accuracy beats reach-chasing: five tags
  that describe the post honestly tell the algorithm who to show it to.
- **Captions, alt text and on-screen text in reels are all indexed.** Alt text
  is a discovery input, not only an accessibility one. Write it properly.
- **An originality score penalises recycled and watermarked clips.** Do not
  repost anything with a TikTok watermark. Original footage only.
- **Small accounts are actively boosted** in the current model. Follower count
  does not gate distribution, so there is no threshold to grind towards before
  the content starts working.

## Hashtag pattern

Two fixed, three variable. The two fixed ones are the account's identity and
appear on everything. The three variable ones describe this specific post.
Never pad to five with something vague.

The fixed pair:

- **#coltriever** — the cross. Measured 30 Aug 2026: 34,419 posts against
  #gollie's 25,684 and #goldenborderretriever's 6,133. It is also the *cleanest*
  of the three: #gollie is diluted by accounts with nothing to do with the
  cross, because "gollie" is a word and a name, while #coltriever returns this
  cross and little else. And owners self-identify with it, which #gollie owners
  mostly do not: @bruce_the_coltriever, @lunacoltriever,
  @bonniedogthecoltriever, @freya.the.coltriever, @sylviethecoltriever,
  @phoenix_coltriever.
  (This corrects an earlier call for #gollie made before the data existed. The
  worry that "coltreiver" would split the tag was wrong: that spelling has
  1,174 posts against 34,419.)
- **#puppydiaries** — the angle, with a caveat. 67,285 posts, but the tag is
  polluted: its single highest-play post in the pull was unrelated Turkish
  comedy. #puppydiary (36,738) is smaller and cleaner if that starts to matter.

**Neither fixed tag is where reach comes from.** The whole breed niche tops out
around 8,000 followers and nobody owns it. That is the opportunity, but it also
means the identity tags are for being *found by the right people* and for
finding accounts to trade shares with. Reach comes from the three variable
slots, which should usually be drawn from the big tags: #newpuppy (1.5M),
#puppytraining (2.9M), #goldenretrieverpuppy (7.3M), #bordercolliemix (1.2M).

**The breed name is a mention, not the identity** (owner decision). Body copy,
headings, page titles and site metadata all say "Golden Retriever × Border
Collie", because that is what someone types when they do not know the
portmanteau. "Gollie" appears in the hashtag block and once in the bio. That is
the whole of it. Do not build captions around the word.

## Cadence

Five or six posts a week, at least three of them reels. Native in-app
scheduling holds posts up to 75 days ahead, 25 a day, so a missed day is
recoverable without breaking the run.

## The thing automation cannot do

Find ten accounts of a similar size in the same niche and trade story shares
weekly. That is the single most reliable way accounts under 100k grow, and no
part of this skill substitutes for it. Flag it if the owner has not done it.
