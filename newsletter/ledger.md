# Issues sent

One row per issue that has gone out. Append with the `newsletter` skill's
`log` mode, or by hand.

It does two jobs: it sets the cutoff date the next `draft` counts forward
from, and it stops a journal entry being used as the spine of two issues
running.

`Covers` is the fortnight the issue drew on. `Sources` is every entry the
letter actually used, not just the ones that got a card.
