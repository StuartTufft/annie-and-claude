# Posted

One row per post that has gone live. Append with the `social` skill's `log`
mode, or by hand.

It does three jobs: stops the idea generator proposing the same angle twice,
records which journal entries have been mined and which are still fresh, and
holds the permalinks needed to embed a reel back into its journal entry.

`pillar` is one of: daily, thenvsnow, firsts, lesson, justthedog.
