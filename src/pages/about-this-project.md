This site is two things at once.

First, it's a record of raising Annie — the training, the setbacks, the ordinary daily stuff — kept mostly so her humans stay honest about how it's actually going, and secondarily because dog stories are just good to read.

Second, it's a small, genuine experiment in using Claude (Anthropic's AI) as a working collaborator rather than a novelty. Here's how a post actually gets made:

1. **Dictate** — a quick voice recap of the day, most days.
2. **Draft** — Claude turns that into a readable post, in the established voice, and strips anything that shouldn't be public.
3. **Review** — a human reads it and either approves it or sends it back. Every post, no exceptions.
4. **Publish** — once approved, it's committed here.
5. **Build & deploy** — happens automatically from there.

Nothing on this site was written or approved without a person actually reading it first. If that ever changes, it'll say so.

The training approach behind it is positive-reinforcement based, welfare-first, and deliberately unfussy — built for someone who works full-time and wants a dog with a strong, trusting relationship, not a checklist of tricks.
