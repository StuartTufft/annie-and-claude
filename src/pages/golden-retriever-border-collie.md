---
title: What a Golden Retriever × Border Collie is actually like
description: Whether she's more collie or retriever, how much energy the cross really needs, and what surprised us most. Written while raising one, not researched.
---

I don't think many people know this cross exists. When we tell someone what
Annie is, a Golden Retriever crossed with a Border Collie, the reaction is
almost always the same: that's going to be a lively one, that's a lot of
energy.

So here's what it has actually been like, from someone in the middle of it
rather than someone who has read about it.

## Is she more collie or retriever?

Honestly, it's still being decided. It's a crazy one.

More collie shows through than I expected. But the physical need to be touching
someone as much as possible is very much retriever.

Fetch is where you see both at once. She picked it up immediately, back and
forth, back and forth, with nobody teaching her anything. I think that's the
collie wanting a job to do and the retriever wanting to please you, arriving at
the same place from two different directions.

## "That's going to be a lot of dog"

Everyone says it. Lively breed, needs a lot of energy.

What I've found is that as long as you keep her entertained, direct it, and
give her a job to do, she's super, super chill. The energy on its own isn't the
problem. Energy with nowhere to go is.

The job right now is small. In these early stages it's biting on the right
things, and not my sofa or my shoes. That's genuinely the whole of it, and it's
enough.

## The thing that surprised me most

How easygoing she is.

I thought socialisation would have to be really, really slow. I'd braced for
that. Instead she's happy to just sit and watch, as long as she's close by to
her humans, and she'll happily take food the whole time she's doing it.

That's the biggest surprise, and if I'd known one thing before we collected
her, it would have been that.

## The joke we keep making

Some days I say we've been missold a land shark. Other days, that we've been
missold a German Shepherd, because she does look very much like one.
